SafeBelt v6: model, questions and safe to spend

Synthetic customers, each checked on their own payday. The model predicts each recurring bill (P, A, T, C); a separate step asks the customer a few questions; a third step turns the bills into a safe-to-spend amount until the next payday (balance − upcoming bills until next payday, minus a safety buffer of 20% of that). Everything is checked against the truth table and what really happened.

Bills per payee (how payments to one payee are split into separate bills)

Each curve is the spread of past amounts to one payee (log scale). Dots are the bill levels; payments to a person are split at the purple lines into separate bills, e.g. rent and utilities to the same landlord. The split is chosen by a Bayesian model that scores both the amounts and the payment dates: a payee is split only if each part has its own amount level and its own regular schedule.

Safe to spend until next payday

Each bill reserves A_P90 ÷ cycle days (rounded down) × (days to next payday + 1), and at least one full A_P90 when it is due before payday. Spendable = balance − upcoming bills until next payday. Safety buffer = 20% of spendable. Safe to spend = spendable − buffer.
